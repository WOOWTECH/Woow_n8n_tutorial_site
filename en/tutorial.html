Woow n8n Onboarding Guide

Hand your repetitive work over to automation

Start by opening Woow n8n, using someone else's template, and building your first workflow. Then continue through expressions, the Code node, AI Agent, and Webhook. There are 22 chapters + 3 appendices, each taking 10 minutes. Find the Sales Handbook, Prompt Library, and Skill Handbook in the Resource Hub.
