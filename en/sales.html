Why your company needs n8n

Every department has repetitive work that someone does but nobody should have to do: manually copying orders from system A to spreadsheet B, routing customer questions by hand, or opening Excel at 8:00 every morning to paste yesterday’s results into a report. These tasks consume 1–2 hours a day, or 300 staff hours a year.

These tasks have three things in common: clear rules, data already in systems, and repeated actions. That is where n8n fits: represent the rules as a workflow, let n8n run it around the clock, and review the reports periodically to confirm it is healthy.

Concept: Like Zapier and Make, n8n lets you connect nodes visually. Unlike them, its source is available under a fair-code license: you can self-host it, retain your data, avoid a fixed monthly plan with usage-based infrastructure, and integrate any API. At the source snapshot its GitHub repository showed more than 130k stars; check the repository for the current count and activity.

What n8n is

n8n (pronounced “n-eight-n”) is a source-available, fair-code workflow automation engine. On its canvas, connect a Trigger to action nodes to create a workflow:

  • A trigger can be a schedule (8:00 every morning), a webhook (someone submits a form), or a system event (Gmail receives a message).
  • An action is what happens next: query Google Sheets, post to Slack, create a Notion page, or send an HTTP request to an internal system.
  • Data flows from the first node to the last. Along the way, a workflow can evaluate conditions, branch, merge, and transform formats.

At the source snapshot, official and community catalogs together listed more than 2,500 integrations and nodes (Slack, Gmail, Notion, Airtable, Google Workspace, Salesforce, Stripe, LINE, Telegram, Discord, AI Agent, and more); check the live catalog because the total changes. For a service without a ready-made node, use HTTP Request to call its API.

Tip: You do not need to master those details. Both options hide the n8n implementation. Tell WoowTech what you want to automate, and we handle the rest.

FAQ

How is pricing calculated?
Both options have a one-time setup fee plus a monthly fee, including maintenance and N workflow changes. Rates depend on the number of workflows, expected execution volume, and SLA tier. [PRICING, CURRENCY, AND QUOTE CHANNEL TBD]
Can I change a workflow myself?
Yes. Both options give you full n8n access, so you can learn to modify workflows or build new ones. WoowTech is available when you do not want to—or do not know how to—make a change. To learn, read the site’s 22-chapter n8n Onboarding Guide.
What is the Option B credentials pool, and how does it differ from my own Slack/OpenAI accounts?
With separate SaaS accounts, you pay each subscription and manage each quota. In the credentials pool, WoowTech obtains commercial accounts for several customers and allocates costs by actual usage. This can reduce costs for low-volume users and consolidate billing. You may opt out and use dedicated accounts.
Can I start with Option B and move to Option A later?
Yes. A common path is to trial the managed option for one month and verify that its workflows improve KPIs. Then export sensitive workflows as JSON to your HAOS add-on. WoowTech assists with data migration and reconnecting credentials.
Does WoowTech use n8n Community or Enterprise Edition? Are features missing?
This repository records Community Edition v2.32.7; confirm the running version because the instance follows community upgrades, which supports typical small and midsize business needs, including AI Agent, custom nodes, sub-workflows, and error workflows. Enterprise-only features such as LDAP/SAML SSO, Advanced RBAC, and version-control integration can be addressed through a separate hybrid deployment.
Can a company without an IT department adopt it?
Yes. That is the purpose of this service. WoowTech covers requirements, workflow development, deployment, and maintenance. You need only one point of contact.
We are comfortable with Zapier. Is n8n worth switching to?
Consider two questions: (1) Does Zapier cost more than USD 100 per month? (2) Do you need something it cannot do, such as connecting an internal system or looping hundreds of times? If either answer is yes, discuss the options. Almost every existing Zap can be rebuilt in n8n, often with more flexibility.
